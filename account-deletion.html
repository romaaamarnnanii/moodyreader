Your data

Delete your account

You can delete your moodyreader account and all of your data at any time. Here’s how, and exactly what gets removed.

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open moodyreader and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Permanently delete.

Your account and data are removed immediately, and you’re signed out.

Or request it by email

Prefer us to do it? Email hello@trymoodyreader.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll remove your account and confirm, typically within a few days.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is permanently deleted:

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your profile and reading preferences
  • All shelves, books you’ve tracked, ratings and reviews
  • Your reading history, goals, streaks and stats

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We don’t keep a backup of your account data after deletion. Anonymous, aggregate records that don’t identify you are not personal data and are unaffected.

Questions?

Email hello@trymoodyreader.com — we’re happy to help.
