02 · Mascot

Moody

A feminine, cute, gloriously clumsy reading ghost — the face of your for-you page.

  • DOKeep her ink outline (#191026) and cream body. Let her be off-balance — that's the charm.
  • DOSwap her face & props for different moods (reading, sobbing, cozy, spooked).
  • DON'TRecolor her outline, stretch her, or make her perfectly upright and tidy.
  • DON'TAdd photoreal shadows — she lives in the flat, hard-shadow sticker world.

06 · Voice

A well-read friend, not a librarian

We sound like…

  • Warm, witty, a little opinionated
  • Transparent — always show why
  • Second person, plain and confident
  • "For people who take books seriously, not solemnly."

We never sound like…

  • Corporate or salesy
  • A black-box algorithm
  • Snobby or gatekeep-y about taste
  • Hype-y startup buzzwords
