Logo notes
----------
- The two "o"s in moody are MOOD DOTS. In product they shift through the
  mood colors (grape/coral/sun/mint); as a static logo, grape + coral is the default.
- The wordmark SVGs use a webfont via @import, so open them in a browser (online)
  to see them correctly. For print / production, convert the text to OUTLINES
  in your vector tool so the logo never depends on the font being installed.
- Keep clear space around the wordmark of at least the height of one mood dot.
- Don't recolor Moody's outline (always ink #191026) or stretch her.

Voice
-----
Warm, witty, literate, a little opinionated — a well-read friend, not a librarian.
Always transparent about how recommendations work. Bold & vibrant, but a little haunted.
